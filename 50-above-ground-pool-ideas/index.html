50 Above Ground Pool Ideas

AN
Curated by AnnaDeco Studio
⏱ 6 min read · Updated 2026-05-10 · ✓ Editorial Verified
📌 Save Idea

Are you tired of that big metal circle sticking out in your yard?

You want a pool, but you don’t want it to look cheap.

Don’t worry, you can make your above ground pool look like a luxury resort without spending a fortune.

I’ve rounded up the best above ground pool ideas to help you get that custom look today.

1. Build A Full Pool Deck

This deck makes your pool feel totally permanent and high-end. It gives you space for seating and décor, improving both access and safety for everyone.

  • Use the deck for sun loungers and dining sets.
  • Ensure the deck railing matches your home style.

Pro Tip: A wrap-around deck immediately hides the pool’s metal structure.

2. Deck Zoning With Levels

Use different deck heights to separate swimming areas from lounging spots. This is great for sloped yards and adds visual interest easily.

  • Design the highest level for sunbathing.
  • Use the lower level for quick pool access.

Pro Tip: Multi-level decks make small yards feel bigger and more complex.

3. Partially Sink The Pool

Digging the pool halfway down lowers the profile significantly. It gives you that natural, in-ground look without the huge cost commitment.

  • This works best on level ground.
  • Check local regulations for semi-buried pools first.

Pro Tip: You only need a short set of steps instead of a tall ladder.

4. Use Durable Composite Decking

Composite materials are perfect around water because they resist moisture, fading, and splinters. It means way less maintenance over the summer.

  • Choose gray or warm brown composite boards.
  • Install hidden fasteners for a clean look.

Pro Tip: Composite decking stays cooler underfoot than wood.

5. Corner Deck Makes Entry Easy

Forget climbing straight up the side. A small corner deck gives you a perfect, stable landing spot right before you dive in. It makes your pool feel much more like an in-ground experience without the massive construction mess.

  • Ensure the deck structure is built using weather-resistant materials like pressure-treated wood.
  • Install non-slip pool steps that lead directly from the deck into the water.
  • Add a secure gate or safety fence to the deck entrance if you have small kids or pets.

Pro Tip: Hide the pool pump and filter right underneath the deck structure to keep your entire backyard looking tidy and professional.

6. Pool On A Raised Platform

Placing the pool on a raised platform deck creates a modern look. This setup also helps improve drainage, especially if your yard is uneven.

  • This works well for very modern homes.
  • Ensure the platform is structurally sound.

Pro Tip: The height gives you a better view of your backyard while swimming.

7. Install Paver Patio Surround

Stone or concrete pavers create a solid, clean, patio-style finish. This makes the pool area feel much more permanent and finished.

  • Choose large format pavers for a sophisticated look.
  • Ensure the ground is level before installing.

Pro Tip: Dark gray pavers hide dirt and look very custom.

8. Soften Edges With Curves

Design your deck with curves instead of sharp corners. This softens the pool’s round shape and gives your yard a custom, organic look.

  • Curves are safer for kids running around.
  • Use flexible composite materials for easier curving.

Pro Tip: Curved designs are great for fitting around existing trees.

9. Hidden Deck Storage Spot

Use the empty space beneath the pool deck for storage. You can hide pumps, chemicals, and all those bright plastic pool toys.

  • Install a small access door for the pump.
  • Use waterproof bins for storing towels and gear.

Pro Tip: This keeps your entire yard clutter-free and tidy.

10. Deck With Built-In Seating

Incorporate benches right into the deck structure itself. This saves space and gives you instant seating without needing extra furniture.

  • Add weather-resistant cushions for comfort.
  • Build the benches wide enough for lounging.

Pro Tip: Built-in seating means you never have to worry about wind blowing chairs away.

11. Resort Style Tropical Plants

Bring the vacation vibes home by planting tropical greenery like palms or banana trees. This instantly creates a resort-style atmosphere.

  • Choose plants that handle full sun well.
  • Use potted plants if you live in a cold climate.

Pro Tip: Focus on large, leafy plants for the best effect.

12. Define Area With Mulch

Use mulch and flower beds to create a clear border around the pool. This visually separates the pool zone from the lawn easily.

  • Use dark mulch for a rich, clean look.
  • Avoid using red-dyed mulch near the pool liner.

Pro Tip: Mulch helps keep grass clippings out of the pool water.

13. Easy Succulent Pool Garden

Succulents and drought-tolerant plants are clean and require almost no effort. This gives you a modern look with zero weeding stress.

  • They thrive in hot, sunny spots.
  • Use white gravel around them for a desert aesthetic.

Pro Tip: These plants don’t attract bees or shed many leaves into the pool.

14. Modern Ornamental Grasses

Plant tall grasses around the pool edge to soften the hard lines. They give an airy, modern feel without being messy like flowers.

  • Choose non-invasive varieties.
  • Plant them in large groups for drama.

Pro Tip: Grasses sway gently in the breeze, adding movement to the space.

15. Beach Vibe Gravel Base

Use pea gravel or sand around the pool for a relaxed, coastal feel. It’s a budget-friendly way to achieve a vacation look.

  • Use landscaping fabric underneath to prevent weeds.
  • Add large driftwood pieces for décor.

Pro Tip: Gravel provides excellent drainage and prevents muddy spots.

16. Use Oversized Pool Planters

Frame the pool with really big planters. They add height, color, and flexibility, especially if your space is paved or on a deck.

  • Choose tall, narrow planters for a contemporary feel.
  • Fill them with bright annual flowers.

Pro Tip: Planters can be moved around if you change your mind about the layout.

17. Shade With A Pergola

Install a pergola or gazebo nearby to define a seating or dining area. This provides much-needed shade and makes the space feel complete.

  • Hang sheer outdoor curtains for extra privacy.
  • Use the structure to hang string lights.

Pro Tip: A pergola clearly separates the ‘lounge’ area from the ‘swim’ area.

18. Fragrant Herb Garden

Plant herbs like lavender, mint, or rosemary close by. They add a great natural scent and you can use them in your summer cooking!

  • Mint is great for making summer drinks.
  • Rosemary is a natural mosquito repellent.

Pro Tip: Planting aromatic herbs keeps the area smelling fresh.

19. Trellis For Privacy Greenery

Use trellises mounted near the pool deck for climbing vines. This adds privacy and greenery without taking up valuable ground space.

  • Grow fast-climbing jasmine for fragrance.
  • Paint the trellis a dark color to make the plants pop.

Pro Tip: This is a cheap way to add height and hide neighbor views.

20. Modern Rock Garden Border

Combine decorative rocks and simple plants like succulents for a clean, modern border. This works well with contemporary backyard styles.

  • Use smooth river stones for a polished look.
  • Arrange the rocks in groups of odd numbers.

Pro Tip: This border requires zero watering or weeding.

21. Add Ambient Night Lighting

Ambient lighting improves safety when swimming after dark. It also creates a super relaxing, resort-like nighttime atmosphere.

  • Avoid bright, harsh floodlights.
  • Focus lights downward onto pathways.

Pro Tip: Warm-toned lights (yellow/orange) are the most soothing.

22. Easy Solar Path Lights

Solar-powered lights are cheap and require zero wiring installation. Use them to light up pathways and deck edges easily and safely.

  • Place them where they get full sun during the day.
  • Look for lights that cast a warm glow.

Pro Tip: Solar lights turn on automatically when the sun goes down.

23. Install A Simple Shade Sail

Hang a large shade sail over the pool or a seating area. This protects swimmers from harsh direct sun during the hottest part of the day.

  • Choose a neutral color like cream or gray.
  • Make sure it’s tautly secured to posts or the house.

Pro Tip: Shade sails are cheaper and easier to install than traditional awnings.

24. Comfortable Lounge Seating

Invest in quality outdoor sofas or comfortable loungers. Creating a dedicated relaxation zone makes the whole area feel instantly higher quality.

  • Choose furniture made of durable wicker or aluminum.
  • Use washable, fade-resistant cushion covers.

Pro Tip: Arrange seating like an outdoor living room, facing the pool.

25. Simple Built-In Pool Bar

Add a small counter or bar area near the water for entertaining. It’s perfect for serving snacks, drinks, and keeping guests happy.

  • Build the bar top using composite material.
  • Add a few high stools for seating.

Pro Tip: A bar makes the pool area the perfect spot for summer parties.

26. Create A Privacy Screen

Use decorative wood panels, screens, or lattice fencing to block views from neighbors. This makes your pool experience feel much more intimate.

  • Screens can be stained to match your deck.
  • Use vertical wood slats for a modern look.

Pro Tip: Place screens strategically where you need the most coverage.

27. Safe Wide Non-Slip Steps

Install wider steps with a non-slip surface. This is a must for safety, especially for kids, pets, and older guests accessing the pool.

  • Ensure steps are securely mounted to the deck.
  • Use textured paint on wood steps for grip.

Pro Tip: Wide steps feel much more welcoming than a narrow ladder.

28. Hidden Storage Benches

Choose benches that have built-in hidden storage inside. They keep towels and toys organized and out of sight when not in use.

  • Look for waterproof lids.
  • Store sunscreen and goggles inside.

Pro Tip: Storage benches are dual-purpose and save you money on a separate shed.

29. Strategic Natural Shade

Plant trees that provide good shade without dropping tons of leaves directly into the water. This saves on your maintenance time.

  • Choose evergreen varieties.
  • Plant them a good distance from the pool edge.

Pro Tip: Look for trees with deep root systems that won’t damage the deck.

30. Simple Rinse Station Shower

Adding a basic outdoor shower keeps dirt, grass, and sunscreen out of the pool water. It’s a simple upgrade that keeps the pool cleaner.

  • Install a simple post shower with a hose connection.
  • Use bamboo screening for privacy around the shower.

Pro Tip: A cold rinse before swimming is a must for water quality.

31. Hide The Pool Frame

Use wood, composite, or vinyl panels to hide the pool’s metal exterior frame. This instantly gives it a custom, polished appearance.

  • Match the skirting color to your deck.
  • Ensure there is good airflow around the frame.

Pro Tip: Skirting is the quickest way to make an above ground pool look custom.

32. Faux Stone Pool Walls

Apply stone veneer or decorative tile to the visible pool walls. This is a huge upgrade that makes the above ground structure look custom built.

  • Use lightweight faux stone panels.
  • Choose natural earth tones for the stone.

Pro Tip: The texture of stone veneer adds depth and luxury.

33. Use Neutral Color Palette

Stick to calming, timeless colors like gray, beige, black, or crisp white. Neutrals always feel modern and sophisticated, never trendy.

  • Use gray for decking and black for fencing.
  • Add color through accessories like towels and pillows.

Pro Tip: Neutral colors make your yard feel bigger and cleaner.

34. Add A Painted Accent

Paint surrounding walls or fences near the pool to add personality. A pop of deep blue or calming green works wonders.

  • Use exterior paint designed for moisture.
  • A deep navy blue looks great with white trim.

Pro Tip: Paint is the cheapest and easiest way to refresh a space.

35. Soften Edges With Fabric

Use removable fabric skirts around the pool base instead of hard panels. This softens the look and lets you change the style seasonally.

  • Choose outdoor canvas fabric that resists mold.
  • Look for Velcro attachments for easy removal.

Pro Tip: Fabric skirts are perfect for hiding uneven ground beneath the pool.

36. Light Up Deck Steps

Install small LED lights along the edges of deck steps. This is a safety feature that also gives a very high-end, contemporary nighttime feel.

  • Use low-voltage wiring for safety.
  • Choose recessed lights that don’t stick out.

Pro Tip: Lighting up steps helps prevent trips and falls after dark.

37. Calming Water Feature

Incorporate a simple fountain or small water spout nearby. The sound of moving water is incredibly relaxing and masks neighborhood noise.

  • Choose a self-contained, solar-powered fountain.
  • Place the feature away from the pool to avoid splash-in.

Pro Tip: The gentle sound of falling water is instantly calming.

38. Cozy Outdoor Textiles

Use weather-resistant rugs and comfortable cushions in your seating areas. This makes the deck feel just as cozy as your living room.

  • Choose rugs specifically labeled for outdoor use.
  • Bring cushions inside during heavy rain.

Pro Tip: A large rug grounds the seating area and defines the space.

39. Match Planters To Deck

Choose planters that use materials matching your deck or patio (stone, wood, or composite). This creates a cohesive, designed look.

  • Use wooden planters on a wooden deck.
  • Paint plastic planters to match your trim color.

Pro Tip: Matching materials makes the space look professionally planned.

40. Warm Rustic Wood Style

Add touches of reclaimed wood or rustic elements to the décor. This creates a warm, natural poolside style that feels cozy.

  • Use reclaimed wood for a towel rack.
  • Look for rustic lanterns and wooden trays.

Pro Tip: Mix rustic wood with modern metals for a balanced look.

41. Install A Swim Jet

A compact swim jet system allows you to swim laps against a current, even in a small pool. It’s a great way to use the pool for exercise.

  • Jets are often adjustable for different speeds.
  • This is perfect for fitness fanatics.

Pro Tip: This turns a small splash pool into a functional lap pool.

42. Fun Pool Game Accessories

Add volleyball nets, basketball hoops, or floating games. This makes the pool more engaging and fun for families and guests.

  • Choose games that are easy to set up and take down.
  • Look for durable, non-rusting materials.

Pro Tip: Keep pool accessories in a dedicated storage bin nearby.

43. Luxurious Pool Floats

Large, stylish floats and loungers enhance comfort and look great when the pool isn’t being used. Look for neutral colors for a chic look.

  • Choose floats shaped like daybeds or chairs.
  • Avoid neon colors for a high-end feel.

Pro Tip: Use an electric pump to inflate large floats quickly.

44. Shallow Splash Zone

If you have room, create a small, shallow play area next to the main pool. This is perfect and safer for younger children to enjoy the water.

  • Use soft rubber tiling in the splash zone.
  • Add small, fun spray features.

Pro Tip: A splash zone keeps toddlers safely separated from the deeper water.

45. Dedicated Towel Station

Install hooks, racks, or a cute standing ladder nearby. This keeps towels dry, organized, and easily accessible for everyone.

  • Choose a rust-proof metal rack.
  • Keep a basket nearby for dirty, wet towels.

Pro Tip: A designated towel spot prevents wet towels from being tossed everywhere.

46. Automatic Pool Cleaner

A robotic cleaner handles the daily maintenance for you. This means less time scrubbing and more time relaxing in crystal clear water.

  • Choose a model designed specifically for above ground pools.
  • Run the cleaner overnight for clear water in the morning.

Pro Tip: This is the best way to save time and keep your water sparkling.

47. Extend Season With Heat

Adding a heater lets you use your pool earlier in the spring and later into the fall. It’s a worthy investment for maximizing pool time.

  • Look at solar heating options for a budget win.
  • Always use a pool cover to retain the heat.

Pro Tip: A heater means you can enjoy swimming when everyone else is still waiting for summer.

48. Festive String Lighting

Hang simple string lights overhead or along the fence line. They instantly create a festive, welcoming mood perfect for evening swims.

  • Use LED bulbs for energy efficiency.
  • Hang them in a zig-zag pattern overhead.

Pro Tip: String lights add romance and make the area feel like a destination.

49. Outdoor Mini Fridge

Place a small, weather-safe fridge nearby for drinks and snacks. This saves you from running back inside every time you need a cold drink.

  • Choose a unit rated for outdoor use.
  • Keep it stocked with bottled water and juice boxes.

Pro Tip: This is a must-have for summer entertaining and BBQs.

50. Pool House Changing Area

Build a compact structure for changing clothes, storage, or just getting out of the sun. It completes the resort setup perfectly.

  • A small cabana can hold patio furniture during winter.
  • Install a hook and mirror inside for changing.

Pro Tip: A pool house makes your backyard feel like a five-star hotel.

See? Your above ground pool doesn’t have to look like a temporary plastic tub.

By adding smart decking, strategic landscaping, and the right accessories, you can create a gorgeous, high-end backyard retreat.

Pick two or three ideas from this list and start building your perfect summer oasis today!
